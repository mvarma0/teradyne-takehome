Meeting: Volta-7 Production Ramp — Capacity & Supply Readiness
Date: 2026-05-20 Time: 09:00 AM CST Location: Conference Room Pecan / Zoom
Attendees: Derek Cole (NPI Program Manager), Nadia Rahman (Supply Chain Manager), Wei Lim (Penang Fab Operations Manager), Lisa Park (PE Manager), Kevin Nash (CFO)
Meeting Type: Supply Chain

Discussion
Derek Cole: NovaDrive's interim PSW lets us ramp in June. Their forecast is 1.2 million units over 18 months, starting near 150,000 in June and reaching 200,000 a month by August. Can the supply chain carry that?
Wei Lim: Wafers are fine. At 82% yield and about 1,100 good die per wafer, 200,000 units needs around 185 wafer starts a month. Fab 2 runs about 800 starts a month in total, and we are committing 250 of them to Volta-7 to cover scrap and safety stock.
Nadia Rahman: The constraint is the QFN-48 wettable-flank leadframe. Our only qualified supplier, Sanwa Leadframe, has allocated us 120,000 frames a month through July.
Kevin Nash: So we are short 80,000 units a month for at least two months. What does a second source cost?
Nadia Rahman: Meridian Packaging in Johor can supply 150,000 frames a month at a 6% higher unit price, but they need PPAP-level qualification: MSL3 preconditioning, temperature cycling and C-SAM on three assembly lots.
Lisa Park: Kevin Tran can run that alongside the remaining HTOL time. It's the same stress hardware.
Kevin Nash: A 6% leadframe premium is under half a cent per unit. Losing 80,000 units of NovaDrive revenue a month is not an option. Approved.
Derek Cole: Test capacity next. At 3.14 seconds with quad-site final test, each tester does roughly 4,500 units an hour, so one tester on one shift covers more than 200,000 a month. Final test is not the bottleneck.
Wei Lim: I'll keep Tester 06 as a hot spare and move Tester 04 to production on June 1.
Nadia Rahman: I also want a 30-day finished-goods buffer at the Penang hub before we commit to NovaDrive's weekly delivery schedule.
Kevin Nash: Fund the buffer. Working capital is fine at this volume.

Decisions
1. Approved second-source qualification of Meridian Packaging QFN-48 leadframes to close the 80,000 unit/month gap.
2. Committed 250 wafer starts/month at Penang Fab 2 and a 30-day finished-goods safety stock for the NovaDrive ramp.

Action Items
Nadia Rahman: Issue the Meridian Packaging qualification purchase order and secure July leadframe allocation — Due: 2026-05-27
Kevin Tran: Run MSL3 preconditioning, TC and C-SAM on three Meridian leadframe assembly lots — Due: 2026-07-10
Wei Lim: Release Tester 04 to production and confirm the 250 wafer starts/month plan — Due: 2026-06-01

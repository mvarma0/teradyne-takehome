Anna Becker: We can submit D1 through D5 by April 7th, detailing the problem description, containment, root cause, and the Rev B mask tape-out commitment, with D6 validation pending silicon re-qualification.
Mike Chen: Bottom line: we tell NovaDrive the truth. Rev A is dead for production. Rev B mask modification is being kicked off right now. We will expedite wafer processing through Penang on hot-lot status.
Tom Harris: What date do I give them for Rev B silicon delivery?
Lisa Park: Derek and Sara estimate four weeks for mask tooling and fab cycle time. That puts Rev B silicon in Austin on April 21.
Mike Chen: Tell Marcus Vance April 21 for Rev B silicon, and full 1000-hour retest data by late April using an accelerated 168-hour / highly stressed screening protocol, followed by production sign-off.
Anna Becker: I will take the lead on drafting the official 8D report. Tom, coordinate all customer communications through me.

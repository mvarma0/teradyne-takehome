Meeting Type: Reliability Review

Discussion
Lisa Park: Kevin, the entire executive committee has their eyes on this room. What are the reliability results on Rev B?
Kevin Tran: We pulled 231 units of Rev B (Lot LOT-V7-020) after completing accelerated stress testing, including 168-hour highly accelerated stress screen and parallel 1000-cycle Temperature Cycling and 96-hour HAST.
Anna Becker: And the ATE electrical readout results?
Kevin Tran: 231 out of 231 units PASSED tri-temp electrical screening. Zero failures. Zero parametric drift on Pin 14 and Pin 22 leakage.
Rosa Santos: We also pulled two sacrificial units from the stress chamber and did cross-sectional curve tracing. The redesigned 280µm ESD clamp showed zero leakage degradation, holding solid at less than 10 nanoamperes at 5.5V bias.
Lisa Park: What about Temperature Cycling from -55°C to +150°C?
Kevin Tran: All 77 units passed 1000 cycles with zero delamination and zero wire-bond fatigue under acoustic microscopy (C-SAM).
Anna Becker: This officially closes D6 of our 8D report! Rev B silicon completely resolves the root cause and fulfills all AEC-Q100 Grade 1 qualification requirements.
Rosa Santos: It's the cleanest reliability dataset I've seen on a first revision turnaround.
Lisa Park: Anna, prepare the formal qualification readout document (`reliability_qual_summary.pptx`). NovaDrive's supplier quality audit is scheduled for May 12, and this data will be our centerpiece.
Kevin Tran: I will finalize the reliability matrix xlsx and sign off the qualification certificate tonight.

Decisions

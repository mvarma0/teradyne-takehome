Meeting: Rev B 1000-Hour HTOL Final Readout & Full Qualification
Date: 2026-06-02 Time: 03:00 PM CST Location: Conference Room Pecan (Austin HQ)
Attendees: Kevin Tran (Reliability Engineer), Anna Becker (Quality Manager), Lisa Park (PE Manager), Mike Chen (VP Product Engineering), Tom Harris (Field Application Engineer)
Meeting Type: Reliability Review

Discussion
Mike Chen: This is the last gate. Kevin, the 1000-hour readout.
Kevin Tran: We pulled all 231 Rev B units from Chambers 2 and 3 this morning at 1,000 hours: TJ 150°C, 1.15V core, 5.25V IO. 231 out of 231 passed tri-temp electrical screening. Zero failures.
Anna Becker: And parametric drift?
Kevin Tran: Flat. Pin 14 and Pin 22 leakage moved from 4.2 to 5.1 nanoamperes against a 1 microampere limit. Core IDDQ moved from 14.2 to 15.1 microamperes against a 50 microampere limit.
Lisa Park: Temperature Cycling finished on May 18 with zero fails, and HAST, ELFR and ESD were already closed. That completes the AEC-Q100 Grade 1 matrix.
Anna Becker: Then D6 of the 8D closes today. I'll send NovaDrive the final validation package and close CAR-2026-ND01.
Tom Harris: Marcus Vance asked for this the minute we had it. With this data they convert the interim PSW to a full PSW.
Mike Chen: Bottom line: Volta-7 Rev B is fully qualified. Great work, Kevin. Publish the reliability summary and the test matrix.
Kevin Tran: The summary deck and the reliability test matrix go out tomorrow.

Decisions

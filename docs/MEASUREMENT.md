# Measurement approach: first 30 days

**Metric: verified-answer rate** = verified answers ÷ knowledge questions asked (small talk and blocked prompts excluded). An answer counts as *verified* when the assistant answered it confidently with at least one valid citation and the user did not thumbs-down, reject or correct it. It captures the two things that decide adoption, coverage (can it answer?) and trust (was it right?), and needs no new instrumentation: answer status, citations and feedback are already logged, and its inputs (answer rate, citation validity, negative feedback) are on the Monitoring page. Week 1 sets the baseline; after that I'd track a 7-day rolling rate split by source type and topic, aiming for ≥70% by day 30. Because the rate can rise simply because people stop giving feedback, I'd check it weekly by hand-auditing 20 random verified answers against their sources. Any drop sends the review team to the low-confidence queue to fill the missing content.
